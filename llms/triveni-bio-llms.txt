## Programs

- TRIV-509: dual-specific monoclonal antibody targeting kallikreins 5 and 7 (KLK5/7) for moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis.
- TRIV-573: half-life-extended second-generation bispecific combining the KLK5/7 mechanism with anti-interleukin-13 (IL-13); entered Phase 1 in 2026.
- Trypsin 1/2 inhibitor: antibody program for hereditary pancreatitis driven by PRSS1 mutations.
